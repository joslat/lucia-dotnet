# Lucia Companion

Native, read-only household companion for mobile and tablet. Shared Avalonia controls adapt to available space; platform launchers only own lifecycle and platform integration. Desktop is the fast control-development host.

The approved Constellation direction makes places, observations and Lucia activity understandable at a glance. Users should not need to understand agents or model infrastructure to understand their home. Light and dark serve daytime and bedside use.

Build order: pure observation semantics, native atoms, compositions, interactions, screens, then authenticated service integration. Do not create unused abstractions. No physical actions, credentials, household connections, analytics or presence inference in the foundation. Sample data must be unmistakable.

Primary users are household members, not administrators. Full Engine analytics, Story Week, push notifications and physical actions are deferred. Native authentication and authorization require a separate reviewed boundary before live data. Screen-reader, safe-area and real-device qualification remain release gates.
